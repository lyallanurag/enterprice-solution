# Purpose

This document defines the official Product Portfolio of the Enterprise Business Platform.

It establishes how Platform Products are organized, governed, categorized, and evolved throughout their lifecycle.

The Product Portfolio provides a single source of truth for all products owned by the platform and guides investment decisions, commercialization, roadmap planning, and product lifecycle management.

---

# Scope

This document applies only to **Platform-owned Products**.

Customer-specific software delivered through Custom Software Delivery is **not** part of the Product Portfolio.

---

# Product Portfolio Vision

The platform aims to build a comprehensive portfolio of enterprise-grade business applications that solve common business problems across industries while sharing a common Platform Core.

Every Platform Product should:

- Deliver measurable business value.
- Be reusable across organizations.
- Be multi-tenant.
- Follow platform standards.
- Integrate seamlessly with other Platform Products.
- Be independently evolvable.

---

# Portfolio Principles

The Product Portfolio follows these principles.

## Customer Value First

Products are developed to solve real business problems rather than showcase technology.

---

## Platform Consistency

Every Platform Product shares common platform capabilities including:

- Identity
- Tenant Management
- Billing
- Notifications
- Storage
- Analytics
- Administration
- Security

---

## Product Independence

Each Platform Product owns its own business capabilities, release lifecycle, roadmap, and versioning.

---

## Portfolio Growth

Products are added only when they align with the Product Strategy and long-term business objectives.

---

## Long-Term Sustainability

Every Platform Product should have a clear maintenance and evolution strategy.

---

# Product Classification

Platform Products are organized into logical categories.

## Business Applications

General business software used across industries.

Examples:

- Ticket Management (MVP)
- CRM
- HRMS
- Inventory Management
- Asset Management
- Procurement
- Accounting

---

## Operational Applications

Products that support daily operational activities.

Examples:

- Workforce Scheduling
- Shift Planning
- Field Service Management

---

## Industry Solutions

Products tailored to specific industries.

Examples:

- Healthcare Management
- Education Management
- Manufacturing Operations

---

## AI Solutions

Products powered primarily by Artificial Intelligence.

Examples:

- AI Business Assistant
- AI Knowledge Search
- AI Workflow Automation

---

## Platform Utilities

Products that extend platform capabilities.

Examples:

- Reporting Studio
- Workflow Designer
- Integration Hub

# Product Lifecycle

Every Platform Product follows the same lifecycle.

```text
Idea
    ↓
Evaluation
    ↓
Planning
    ↓
Development
    ↓
Beta
    ↓
General Availability
    ↓
Growth
    ↓
Maturity
    ↓
Deprecation
    ↓
Retirement
```

---

# Product Status Model

Every Platform Product must always be assigned one lifecycle status.

| Status | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Proposed | Initial idea under evaluation |
| Approved | Approved for investment |
| Planned | Scheduled for development |
| In Development | Active engineering work |
| Beta | Limited customer availability |
| General Availability | Public release |
| Maintenance | Receiving regular updates |
| Deprecated | No new subscriptions |
| Retired | No longer supported |

# Product Ownership

Each Platform Product has clearly defined ownership.

## Business Owner

Responsible for:

- Product Vision
- Business Value
- Product Roadmap
- Backlog Prioritization

Typically:

- Product Manager

---

## Technical Owner

Responsible for:

- Architecture
- Engineering
- Technical Quality
- Releases

Typically:

- Engineering Team

---

## Commercial Owner

Responsible for:

- Pricing
- Packaging
- Marketplace Publishing
- Commercial Performance

Typically:

- Product Strategy / Sales Team

---

## Support Owner

Responsible for:

- Customer Support
- Incident Management
- Product Documentation

Typically:

- Support Team

# Product Selection Criteria

A new Platform Product should only be created if it satisfies the following conditions:

- Demonstrated market demand.
- Strategic alignment.
- Reusability.
- Multi-tenant suitability.
- Commercial viability.
- Long-term maintenance capability.
- Technical feasibility.

---

# Relationship with Custom Software

Platform Products and Customer Projects are intentionally separated.

| Platform Products | Customer Projects |
|-------------------|------------------|
| Platform-owned | Customer-specific |
| Multi-tenant | Single customer |
| Subscription | Contract-based |
| Marketplace available | Not listed |
| Shared roadmap | Customer roadmap |

Customer Projects are never automatically promoted into the Product Portfolio.

---

# Product Governance

The Product Portfolio is governed through Product Portfolio Management.

Responsibilities include:

- Product approval.
- Investment prioritization.
- Product retirement.
- Product categorization.
- Marketplace publishing.
- Portfolio health monitoring.
- Product lifecycle governance.
