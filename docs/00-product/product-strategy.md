# Purpose

This document defines the long-term product strategy for the Enterprise Business Platform.

It establishes the strategic direction for the platform's product portfolio, commercial offerings, and investment decisions. It provides the foundation for Business Architecture, Domain-Driven Design (DDD), Product Management, and Engineering.

The strategy ensures that every product developed by the platform aligns with the overall business vision and long-term objectives.

---

# Executive Summary

The Enterprise Business Platform is designed to become a unified ecosystem that enables organizations to acquire, build, and manage business software through a single platform.

Unlike traditional SaaS providers that focus solely on subscription products, the platform combines two complementary business models:

- Platform-owned SaaS Products
- Custom Software Delivery

Organizations may either subscribe to existing Platform Products or commission custom software tailored to their unique business requirements.

Both offerings operate within a shared Platform Core that provides common capabilities such as Identity, Billing, Notifications, Storage, Analytics, Security, and Administration.

---

# Strategic Objectives

The platform pursues the following strategic objectives.

## Build a Strong Product Portfolio

Develop a growing collection of enterprise-grade Platform Products that solve common business problems across multiple industries.

---

## Deliver Custom Solutions

Provide organizations with the ability to request and commission software that does not currently exist within the Platform Product portfolio.

---

## Create a Unified Customer Experience

Allow organizations to manage Platform Products, Custom Projects, Subscriptions, Billing, Support, and Users from a single platform.
