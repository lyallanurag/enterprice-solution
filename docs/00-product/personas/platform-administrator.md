**Status:** Approved

**Last Updated:** July 2026

---

# Purpose

The Platform Administrator is responsible for the operational administration of the Enterprise Business Platform.

This Business Actor ensures that the platform operates securely, reliably, and efficiently while supporting customer organizations, Platform Products, and internal business operations.

The Platform Administrator governs platform configuration and operational policies but does not own business strategy or product direction.

---

# Executive Summary

The Platform Administrator manages the day-to-day operation of the platform.

This includes platform configuration, tenant administration, platform monitoring, operational governance, and coordination with other business actors to ensure reliable platform services.

The Platform Administrator focuses on platform operations rather than infrastructure management or software development.

---

# Business Outcome

Maintain a secure, stable, and well-governed platform that enables organizations to successfully use Platform Products and services.

---

# Business Responsibilities

The Platform Administrator is responsible for:

- Managing platform configuration.
- Managing platform-wide operational settings.
- Overseeing organization onboarding.
- Monitoring platform health.
- Managing tenant lifecycle.
- Managing platform announcements.
- Managing operational policies.
- Coordinating maintenance windows.
- Supporting platform governance.
- Ensuring operational compliance.
- Collaborating with Support Engineers during operational incidents.

---

# Decision Authority

The Platform Administrator may approve:

- Platform configuration changes.
- Organization activation and suspension.
- Platform maintenance schedules.
- Operational platform policies.
- Platform announcements.
- Platform operational settings.

The Platform Administrator does not approve:

- Product Strategy
- Product Pricing
- Commercial Policies
- Product Portfolio Decisions
- Long-term Business Investments

---

# Primary Business Capabilities

The Platform Administrator primarily interacts with:

- Platform Administration
- Identity & Access Management
- Organization Management
- Tenant Management
- Platform Configuration
- Notification Management
- Audit Management
- Operational Monitoring

---

# Products Used

The Platform Administrator commonly uses:

- Administration Console
- Organization Dashboard
- Platform Configuration
- Operational Monitoring Dashboard
- Audit Logs
- Notification Center
- Subscription Overview

---

# Key Interactions

The Platform Administrator collaborates with:

- Platform Owner
- Product Manager
- Finance Manager
- Support Engineer
- Organization Administrators

The Platform Administrator ensures operational continuity across the platform.

---

# Success Metrics

Success is measured through:

- Platform Availability
- Organization Onboarding Success
- Operational Incident Resolution Time
- Platform Configuration Accuracy
- Tenant Provisioning Time
- Operational Compliance
- Customer Operational Satisfaction

---

# Risks

Potential risks include:

- Incorrect platform configuration.
- Tenant provisioning failures.
- Operational outages.
- Security policy misconfiguration.
- Poor operational governance.
- Delayed incident response.

---

# Challenges

Typical challenges include:

- Managing platform growth.
- Supporting increasing numbers of organizations.
- Maintaining operational consistency.
- Coordinating maintenance with minimal disruption.
- Balancing platform flexibility with governance.

---

# Future Evolution

As the platform grows, the Platform Administrator may become responsible for:

- Multi-region platform operations.
- Regional platform administration.
- Platform automation governance.
- Operational policy management.
- AI-assisted platform administration.
