# Purpose

The Support Engineer is responsible for ensuring that customer organizations receive timely, effective, and professional operational support while using the Enterprise Business Platform.

The Support Engineer acts as the primary operational contact for customer assistance, incident coordination, issue resolution, and product feedback.

This Business Actor helps organizations maximize the value of Platform Products and Custom Software solutions throughout their lifecycle.
