# Purpose

The Organization Administrator is the primary business representative of a customer organization within the Enterprise Business Platform.

This Business Actor is responsible for managing the organization's relationship with the platform, including subscriptions, organizational settings, user administration, Platform Product adoption, and Custom Software engagements.

The Organization Administrator ensures that the platform delivers value to their organization while maintaining governance over organizational usage.

---

# Executive Summary

The Organization Administrator is the highest-level business actor within a customer organization.

This actor manages the organization's platform presence, controls access for organization members, oversees subscriptions, initiates custom software requests, and acts as the primary point of contact between the organization and the platform.

---

# Business Outcome

Enable the organization to successfully adopt Platform Products and Custom Software services while ensuring effective governance, operational efficiency, and business value.

---

# Business Responsibilities

The Organization Administrator is responsible for:

- Registering and managing the organization.
- Managing organization profile and settings.
- Managing organizational users.
- Managing subscriptions.
- Purchasing Platform Products.
- Managing product assignments.
- Initiating Custom Software requests.
- Reviewing Delivery Provider proposals.
- Awarding customer projects.
- Monitoring active projects.
- Managing organization billing contacts.
- Reviewing invoices.
- Coordinating with Support Engineers.
- Ensuring platform adoption across the organization.

---

# Decision Authority

The Organization Administrator may approve:

- User invitations.
- User removal.
- Role assignments.
- Product subscriptions.
- Subscription upgrades.
- Custom Software requests.
- Delivery Provider selection.
- Project acceptance.
- Organization profile updates.

The Organization Administrator does not approve:

- Platform governance.
- Platform pricing.
- Platform policies.
- Platform Products.
- Marketplace governance.

---

# Primary Business Capabilities

The Organization Administrator primarily interacts with:

- Organization Management
- Identity & Access Management
- Subscription Management
- Product Marketplace
- Delivery Marketplace
- Project Management
- Billing
- Customer Support
- Notification Management

# Products Used

The Organization Administrator commonly uses:

- Organization Dashboard
- User Management
- Subscription Center

- Delivery Marketplace

# Key Interactions

The Organization Administrator collaborates with:

- Sales Manager
- Finance Manager
- Support Engineer
- Delivery Provider
- Organization Users

The Organization Administrator acts as the primary decision-maker for the organization's relationship with the platform.

---

# Success Metrics

Success is measured through:

- Successful Platform Product adoption.
- Active organization users.
- Efficient subscription management.
- Successful project delivery.
- User satisfaction.
- Organization productivity.
- Platform utilization.
- Renewal success.

---

# Risks

Potential risks include:

- Low platform adoption.
- Poor user governance.
- Incorrect subscription management.
- Delayed project decisions.
- Ineffective communication.
- Weak organizational engagement.

---

# Challenges

Typical challenges include:

- Managing organizational change.
- Encouraging user adoption.
- Selecting the right Platform Products.
- Choosing the right Delivery Provider.
- Balancing business priorities.
- Managing organizational growth.

---

# Future Evolution

As the platform evolves, the Organization Administrator may also manage:

- Multiple business units.
- Regional organizations.
- Cross-organization collaboration.
- AI assistants for organization management.
- Advanced governance policies.
- Compliance reporting.
