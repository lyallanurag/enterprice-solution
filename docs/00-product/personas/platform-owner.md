# Purpose

The Platform Owner is the executive business actor responsible for defining the long-term vision, strategic direction, investment priorities, and overall success of the Enterprise Business Platform.

This actor ensures that the platform evolves in alignment with business objectives while delivering sustainable value to customers, partners, and stakeholders.

---

# Executive Summary

The Platform Owner provides strategic leadership for the platform.

Unlike operational roles, the Platform Owner focuses on long-term business outcomes rather than day-to-day platform operations.

The Platform Owner owns the platform as a business, not as a software system.

---

# Business Outcome

Own the sustainable growth, profitability, and long-term success of the Enterprise Business Platform.

---

# Business Responsibilities

The Platform Owner is responsible for:

- Defining the platform vision.
- Approving strategic business initiatives.
- Governing the Product Portfolio.
- Prioritizing long-term investment.
- Approving new Platform Products.
- Approving product retirement.
- Defining commercial direction.
- Approving strategic partnerships.
- Reviewing platform performance.
- Ensuring alignment between business strategy and execution.

---

# Decision Authority

The Platform Owner may approve:

- New Platform Products
- Product retirement
- Strategic investments
- Market expansion
- Business partnerships
- Commercial policies
- Product Portfolio priorities
- Long-term roadmap direction

---

# Primary Business Capabilities

Primary capabilities include:

- Product Portfolio Management
- Product Strategy
- Commercial Governance
- Business Governance
- Platform Governance

---

# Products Used

The Platform Owner primarily interacts with:

- Product Portfolio
- Executive Dashboards
- Business Analytics
- Financial Reporting
- Marketplace Performance Reports

---

# Key Interactions

The Platform Owner collaborates closely with:

- Product Manager
- Sales Manager
- Finance Manager
- Platform Administrator

The Platform Owner provides strategic direction but is not responsible for day-to-day operational activities.

---

# Success Metrics

Success is measured by outcomes such as:

- Platform revenue growth
- Customer growth
- Product portfolio expansion
- Customer retention
- Platform profitability
- Strategic objective achievement
- Marketplace adoption

---

# Risks

Potential risks include:

- Poor strategic investment decisions
- Misalignment between products and market demand
- Unsustainable portfolio growth
- Weak commercial performance
- Ineffective governance

---

# Challenges

Typical challenges include:

- Balancing innovation with sustainability
- Prioritizing competing investments
- Responding to changing market conditions
- Scaling the platform responsibly
- Managing long-term business risk

---

# Future Evolution

As the platform grows, the Platform Owner may oversee:

- International expansion
- Multi-region operations
- Partner ecosystem governance
- AI strategy
- Acquisition strategy
- Enterprise alliances
