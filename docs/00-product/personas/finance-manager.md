# Purpose

The Finance Manager is responsible for governing the financial operations of the Enterprise Business Platform.

This Business Actor ensures that commercial transactions, subscriptions, invoices, payments, financial reporting, and revenue management are accurate, compliant, and aligned with the platform's commercial strategy.

The Finance Manager supports sustainable business growth through sound financial governance and operational excellence.

---

# Executive Summary

The Finance Manager oversees the financial aspects of Platform Products and Custom Software Delivery.

This includes subscription billing, payment reconciliation, invoicing, revenue reporting, taxation, refunds, financial controls, and commercial compliance.

The Finance Manager collaborates with Sales, Product Management, Platform Administration, and customer organizations to ensure a transparent and efficient financial experience.

---

# Business Outcome

Maintain accurate financial operations while maximizing revenue integrity, ensuring regulatory compliance, and supporting long-term platform sustainability.

---

# Business Responsibilities

The Finance Manager is responsible for:

- Managing subscription billing.
- Managing invoice generation.
- Monitoring payment collection.
- Managing refunds and credit notes.
- Monitoring recurring revenue.
- Financial reporting.
- Revenue reconciliation.
- Tax management.
- Financial compliance.
- Supporting commercial pricing implementation.
- Supporting enterprise contract billing.
- Financial forecasting.

---

# Decision Authority

The Finance Manager may approve:

- Invoice issuance.
- Refunds within policy.
- Credit notes.
- Payment reconciliation.
- Financial reporting.
- Billing policies.
- Financial compliance activities.

The Finance Manager does not approve:

- Product Pricing Strategy.
- Commercial Policies.
- Product Portfolio decisions.
- Product Roadmap.
- Platform Strategy.

---

# Primary Business Capabilities

The Finance Manager primarily interacts with:

- Billing Management
- Payment Management
- Subscription Management
- Financial Reporting
- Revenue Management
- Invoice Management
- Refund Management
- Tax Management

---

# Products Used

The Finance Manager commonly uses:

- Billing Dashboard
- Invoice Management
- Payment Console
- Financial Reporting
- Revenue Analytics
- Subscription Dashboard
- Contract Billing Workspace

---

# Key Interactions

The Finance Manager collaborates with:

- Platform Owner
- Sales Manager
- Product Manager
- Platform Administrator
- Organization Administrators

The Finance Manager ensures that commercial activities are translated into accurate financial operations.

---

# Success Metrics

Success is measured through:

- Billing Accuracy
- Invoice Processing Time
- Payment Collection Rate
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
- Revenue Recognition Accuracy
- Refund Processing Time
- Financial Compliance
- Customer Payment Satisfaction

---

# Risks

Potential risks include:

- Billing errors.
- Revenue leakage.
- Payment failures.
- Financial reporting inaccuracies.
- Tax compliance issues.
- Delayed invoicing.
- Poor cash flow visibility.

---

# Challenges

Typical challenges include:

- Supporting multiple pricing models.
- Managing subscription renewals.
- Handling enterprise billing requirements.
- Maintaining regulatory compliance.
- Supporting multiple currencies (future).
- Balancing automation with financial controls.

---

# Future Evolution

As the platform grows, the Finance Manager may become responsible for:

- Multi-currency financial operations.
- Regional tax management.
- Marketplace commission settlement.
- Partner revenue sharing.
- AI-assisted financial forecasting.
- Financial risk analytics.
