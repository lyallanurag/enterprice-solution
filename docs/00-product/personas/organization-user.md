# Purpose

The Organization User is a business actor who uses Platform Products and services to perform day-to-day business activities within their organization.

This Business Actor represents the primary consumer of business functionality delivered by the Enterprise Business Platform.

Organization Users focus on achieving business outcomes rather than administering the platform or managing organizational governance.

---

# Executive Summary

Organization Users perform operational business work using one or more subscribed Platform Products.

They access the platform through roles assigned by the Organization Administrator and collaborate with colleagues, Delivery Providers, and Support Engineers when necessary.

Their experience directly reflects the value delivered by the platform.

---

# Business Outcome

Complete daily business activities efficiently while maximizing the value of subscribed Platform Products.

---

# Business Responsibilities

The Organization User is responsible for:

- Performing assigned business activities.
- Using subscribed Platform Products.
- Managing personal tasks.
- Collaborating with other organization members.
- Participating in workflows.
- Updating assigned business records.
- Reviewing assigned notifications.
- Reporting issues.
- Providing product feedback.
- Following organizational policies.

---

# Decision Authority

The Organization User may:

- Update personal information.
- Complete assigned business tasks.
- Manage personal preferences.
- Submit support requests.
- Participate in approved workflows.
- View information permitted by assigned roles.

The Organization User does not approve:

- Organization settings.
- User management.
- Product subscriptions.
- Billing.
- Commercial agreements.
- Platform governance.

---

# Primary Business Capabilities

The Organization User primarily interacts with:

- Platform Products
- Workflow Management
- Task Management
- Collaboration
- Notification Management
- Knowledge Management
- Customer Support

Business capabilities available depend on subscribed Platform Products and assigned roles.

---

# Products Used

Examples include:

- Ticket Management
- CRM (Future)
- HRMS (Future)
- Inventory Management (Future)
- Asset Management (Future)
- Knowledge Management (Future)

---

# Key Interactions

The Organization User collaborates with:

- Organization Administrator
- Other Organization Users
- Delivery Providers (when participating in customer projects)
- Support Engineers

---

# Success Metrics

Success is measured through:

- Task completion.
- Product adoption.
- User productivity.
- Workflow completion.
- User satisfaction.
- Collaboration effectiveness.

---

# Risks

Potential risks include:

- Low platform adoption.
- Incomplete workflows.
- Poor user engagement.
- Inaccurate business data.
- Insufficient training.
- Reduced productivity.

---

# Challenges

Typical challenges include:

- Learning new Platform Products.
- Managing multiple business processes.
- Collaborating across teams.
- Adapting to product changes.
- Maintaining data quality.

---

# Future Evolution

As the platform evolves, Organization Users may benefit from:

- AI Business Assistants.
- Intelligent task automation.
- Personalized dashboards.
- Cross-product workflows.
- Advanced collaboration features.
- Voice and conversational interfaces.
