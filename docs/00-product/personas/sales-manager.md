**Document ID:** ACTOR-SALES-MANAGER
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Approved
**Owner:** Product Strategy Team
**Last Updated:** July 2026

---

# Purpose

The Sales Manager is responsible for acquiring new customers, expanding existing customer relationships, and driving sustainable revenue growth across Platform Products and Custom Software Delivery.

The Sales Manager ensures that organizations receive the most appropriate solution for their business needs while maintaining long-term commercial relationships.

---

# Executive Summary

The Sales Manager represents the commercial face of the Enterprise Business Platform.

This Business Actor works closely with prospective and existing customers to understand business challenges, recommend suitable Platform Products, coordinate Custom Software opportunities, and ensure successful commercial engagement.

The Sales Manager focuses on business growth rather than operational product delivery.

---

# Business Outcome

Increase sustainable revenue while building long-term customer relationships and ensuring organizations successfully adopt the platform.

---

# Business Responsibilities

The Sales Manager is responsible for:

- Identifying new business opportunities.
- Managing customer acquisition.
- Qualifying customer requirements.
- Recommending Platform Products.
- Coordinating Custom Software opportunities.
- Preparing commercial proposals.
- Managing enterprise negotiations.
- Coordinating contract initiation.
- Collaborating with Delivery Providers during pre-sales.
- Supporting customer renewals and account growth.

---

# Decision Authority

The Sales Manager may approve:

- Sales proposals.
- Commercial quotations.
- Customer discounts within approved limits.
- Sales opportunities.
- Customer onboarding coordination.
- Enterprise negotiations.

The Sales Manager does not approve:

- Product Pricing Strategy
- Commercial Policies
- Product Portfolio Decisions
- Platform Governance
- Financial Accounting

---

# Primary Business Capabilities

The Sales Manager primarily interacts with:

- Customer Relationship Management
- Product Marketplace
- Delivery Marketplace
- Commercial Management
- Proposal Management
- Contract Management
- Subscription Management

---

# Products Used

The Sales Manager commonly uses:

- CRM Dashboard
- Sales Pipeline
- Product Catalog
- Marketplace Portal
- Proposal Management
- Contract Workspace
- Customer Analytics

---

# Key Interactions

The Sales Manager collaborates with:

- Platform Owner
- Product Manager
- Finance Manager
- Organization Administrator
- Delivery Provider
- Support Engineer

The Sales Manager acts as the primary commercial contact before and during customer engagement.

---

# Success Metrics

Success is measured through:

- New Organizations Acquired
- Revenue Growth
- Conversion Rate
- Proposal Acceptance Rate
- Customer Retention
- Renewal Rate
- Average Contract Value
- Customer Lifetime Value

---

# Risks

Potential risks include:

- Poor customer qualification.
- Low conversion rates.
- Unrealistic customer expectations.
- Revenue decline.
- Weak commercial forecasting.
- Customer churn.

---

# Challenges

Typical challenges include:

- Balancing customer expectations with platform capabilities.
- Managing competitive markets.
- Coordinating multiple stakeholders.
- Maintaining long-term customer relationships.
- Supporting enterprise procurement processes.

---

# Future Evolution

As the platform grows, the Sales Manager may become responsible for:

- Regional sales organizations.
- Partner sales channels.
- Enterprise account management.
- Customer success coordination.
- AI-assisted sales forecasting.
- Marketplace growth initiatives.
