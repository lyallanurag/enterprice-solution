# Purpose

This document defines the planned evolution of the platform.

The roadmap provides strategic direction for business, product, architecture, and engineering teams by organizing development into progressive capability-based phases rather than fixed delivery dates.

The roadmap is intended to evolve as customer needs, market opportunities, and business priorities change.
