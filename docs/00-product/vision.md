# Purpose

This document defines the long-term vision of the Enterprise Business Platform.

It establishes the strategic direction for the platform, aligns business and engineering decisions, and serves as the highest-level reference for product strategy, architecture, and organizational growth.

The vision is intentionally technology-independent and focuses on the long-term purpose of the platform.

---

# Vision Statement

> **To become the most trusted Enterprise Business Platform that enables organizations to acquire, build, manage, and evolve business software through a unified ecosystem of platform products, custom software delivery, and trusted delivery partners.**
