# Purpose

This document defines the pricing principles and commercial pricing models for the Enterprise Business Platform.

The Pricing Strategy ensures that every Platform Product and Custom Software Delivery engagement follows a consistent, transparent, and sustainable pricing approach aligned with the overall business objectives.

It provides guidance for subscription pricing, enterprise licensing, custom project pricing, support offerings, and future commercial expansion.

---

# Executive Summary

The Enterprise Business Platform follows a hybrid pricing model that combines recurring subscription revenue from Platform Products with project-based revenue from Custom Software Delivery.

Pricing is designed to be:

- Transparent
- Flexible
- Scalable
- Competitive
- Sustainable

The pricing strategy supports organizations of all sizes while enabling long-term growth of the platform.

---

# Pricing Principles

The platform follows these pricing principles.

## Customer Value First

Pricing reflects measurable business value rather than technical complexity.

---

## Simplicity

Pricing models should be easy to understand and predictable.

---

## Scalability

Organizations should be able to start small and grow without changing platforms.

---

## Transparency

No hidden costs.

Customers should clearly understand what is included in every commercial offering.

---

## Sustainability

Pricing must support continuous investment in Platform Products, Platform Core, and customer success.

---

# Commercial Pricing Models

The platform supports two independent commercial models.

---

## Platform Products

Platform Products follow recurring subscription pricing.

Supported models include:

- Monthly Subscription
- Annual Subscription
- Enterprise Subscription

Future models may include:

- Usage-based Pricing
- Consumption Pricing
- Add-on Modules
- AI Credits

---

## Custom Software Delivery

Custom Software projects may use different pricing models depending on project requirements.

Supported models include:

- Fixed Price
- Time & Material
- Milestone Based
- Retainer Agreement

Future models:

- Dedicated Development Team
- Managed Delivery
- Outcome-based Pricing

---

# Subscription Strategy

Platform Products should support subscription plans appropriate for different customer segments.

Typical subscription tiers include:

- Starter
- Professional
- Business
- Enterprise

Actual pricing values are managed outside this document and may evolve independently.

---

# Enterprise Licensing

Enterprise customers may negotiate commercial agreements that include:

- Organization-wide licensing
- Volume discounts
- Multi-year contracts
- Dedicated support
- Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
- Premium onboarding

---

# Custom Software Pricing

Every custom software engagement follows a structured estimation process.

Pricing may consider:

- Project complexity
- Estimated effort
- Delivery timeline
- Team composition
- Technology requirements
- Integration complexity
- Support requirements

Pricing is agreed through a formal proposal and contract before development begins.

---

# Marketplace Pricing

## Product Marketplace

Platform Products define their own pricing plans within the platform's commercial policies.

Customers can compare plans and subscribe directly.

---

## Delivery Marketplace

Delivery Providers submit commercial proposals for customer projects.

Proposal pricing is determined by the Delivery Provider based on customer requirements.

The platform does not prescribe pricing for independent Delivery Providers.

---

# Discounts

Discounts may be offered through approved commercial programs.

Examples include:

- Annual subscription discounts
- Volume discounts
- Promotional campaigns
- Educational pricing
- Non-profit pricing
- Strategic partnership agreements

Discount approval follows commercial governance policies.

---

# Billing Alignment

Pricing Strategy is implemented through the Billing capability.

Billing responsibilities include:

- Subscription billing
- Invoice generation
- Payment collection
- Taxes
- Credit notes
- Refunds
- Renewals

---

# Payment Strategy

Supported payment models include:

- Online payments
- Bank transfer
- Enterprise invoicing

Future payment options may include:

- Digital wallets
- Regional payment gateways
- Marketplace escrow (for custom software projects)

---

# Pricing Governance

Pricing decisions are owned by Product Portfolio Management in collaboration with Finance and Sales.

Responsibilities include:

- Product pricing
- Pricing reviews
- Discount policies
- Commercial packaging
- Revenue optimization

---

# Success Metrics

Pricing effectiveness is measured through:

- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Average Revenue per Organization (ARPO)
- Subscription Growth
- Renewal Rate
- Gross Margin
- Project Profitability

---

# Future Strategy

Future pricing capabilities may include:

- AI-based pricing recommendations
- Dynamic pricing
- Regional pricing
- Multi-currency pricing
- Partner revenue sharing
- Marketplace commissions

---

# Strategic Principles

The platform follows these long-term pricing principles.

- Price according to business value.
- Keep pricing transparent.
- Encourage long-term customer relationships.
- Separate product pricing from custom project pricing.
- Maintain flexibility without sacrificing simplicity.
- Ensure sustainable platform growth.
