# Purpose

This document defines the business operating model of the Enterprise Business Platform.

It explains how the platform creates value, delivers value, generates revenue, and connects customers, delivery providers, and platform-owned products within a unified ecosystem.

This document serves as the foundation for Business Architecture, Domain-Driven Design, Product Strategy, and Software Architecture.

---

# Vision of the Business

The platform is an Enterprise Business Platform that combines:

* A Platform Core providing shared platform capabilities.
* A portfolio of Platform-owned SaaS Products.
* A Marketplace for discovering and subscribing to platform products.
* A Custom Software Delivery ecosystem connecting customers with delivery providers.

The platform enables organizations to either subscribe to existing business software or commission custom software tailored to their specific business requirements.

---

# Business Lines

The platform operates through three primary business lines.

## 1. Platform Products

Platform Products are designed, developed, owned, and maintained by the platform owner.

These products are multi-tenant SaaS applications made available through the Marketplace.

### MVP Product

* Ticket Management

### Future Products

* CRM
* Inventory Management
* HRMS
* Asset Management
* Accounting
* Procurement
* Knowledge Management

The product portfolio will evolve based on market demand and strategic direction.
